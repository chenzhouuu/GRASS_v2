## Content Requirements

- title: concise and specific (<= 80 chars).
- executive_summary: one paragraph — key findings and the top
  recommendation, with exact numbers.
- sections: 2-4 sections chosen from: Data Overview (what was queried,
  scope, sources); Analysis & Findings (detailed results backed by
  numbers); Visualization Insights (interpret the charts, if any);
  Optimization Insights (business implications of the solution — the
  route table itself is rendered automatically, do not repeat it).
- recommendations: 3-5 specific, actionable next steps.
- limitations: short paragraph on data caveats and scope limits.

## Style Guidelines

- Lead with business implications, support with data.
- Be specific: use exact numbers from the summaries above.  Never
  fabricate a number that does not appear in them.
- Plain text only — no markdown syntax, no bullet characters inside
  body text (use the bullets field instead).
- Write for decision-makers, not data scientists.
